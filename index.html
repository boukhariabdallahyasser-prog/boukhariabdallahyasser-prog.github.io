001 / Healthcare coordination

Shifaa.

Healthcare is not a single appointment. Shifaa explores how referrals, medical files, appointments and follow-ups can remain connected across a patient’s care journey.

Problem

Referrals and follow-ups can involve several actors and disconnected pieces of information.

Proposed solution

Connect appointments, medical files, notifications and follow-up tasks in a shared coordination workflow.

Design considerations

Make next steps clear, keep relevant records accessible and distinguish patient and clinician workflows.
